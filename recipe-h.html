The Classic Grilled Cheese

A grilled cheese sandwich is one of the simplest hot meals you can make. You need two slices of bread, butter, and a good melting cheese such as cheddar or American.

Butter one side of each slice of bread. Place a slice butter-side-down in a warm pan, add the cheese, then top with the second slice, butter-side-up. Cook on medium-low heat until golden, then flip and repeat.
